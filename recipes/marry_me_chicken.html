Marry Me Chicken

Why you’ll love this marry me chicken recipe

  • So much flavor. Marry me chicken includes a velvety, dreamy sun-dried tomato parmesan cream sauce spooned over the juiciest, golden-brown pan-seared chicken.
  • Speedy comfort food. The entire dish comes together in just 30 minutes – it’s warm, it’s cozy, and it’s restaurant-quality, best served with all the crusty bread to sop up all the saucy bits!
  • Perfect for all occasions. Marry me chicken is perfect for a quick weeknight family meal, a romantic night for two, or for weekend company for a crowd-pleasing dinner with minimal effort.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 teaspoons dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • ¼ teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • ⅓ cup white wine
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • ½ cup julienned sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
  • ¼ cup freshly grated Parmesan
  • 8 chopped fresh basil leaves

Steps

  1. Season chicken with 1 teaspoon salt and 1/2 teaspoon pepper.
  2. Heat olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat. Working in batches, add chicken to the Dutch oven in a single layer and cook until golden brown and cooked through, reaching an internal temperature of 165 degrees F, about 4-5 minutes per side; set aside.
  3. Stir in tomato paste, garlic, oregano, thyme and red pepper flakes until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  4. Whisk in flour until lightly browned, about 1 minute.
  5. Stir in wine, scraping any browned bits from the bottom of the Dutch oven.
  6. Stir in chicken stock, heavy cream and sun-dried tomatoes; season with salt and pepper, to taste.
  7. Bring to a boil; reduce heat and simmer, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened and flavors have blended, about 5 minutes. If the mixture is too thick, add more stock as needed.
  8. Stir in Parmesan and basil. Return chicken to the Dutch oven.
  9. Serve warm.
